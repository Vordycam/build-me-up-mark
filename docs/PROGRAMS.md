# Program catalogue (draft for approval) - what the app ships on day one

Every program carries: goal, level, days/week, gym or home version, block length, progression rule, deload rule, cardio prescription, nutrition targets, and the champion principle it is built on. Each is resolved against the user's equipment and joint flags, so the same template produces a gym and a home session.

## LEAN programs

1. Lean Foundations (beginner, 3 lifting days + 2 cardio, gym or home, 8 weeks)
   Full body A / B / A alternating. A: goblet or back squat 3x10, push-up or bench 3x10, row 3x10, Romanian deadlift 3x10, plank 3x30 s. B: deadlift or hip hinge 3x8, overhead press 3x10, lat pulldown or pull-up ladder 3x8, split squat 3x10, dead bug 3x10. 10-minute finisher EMOM (burpees / KB swings / jump rope). Cardio: 2 Zone 2 walks or jogs 30-40 min (or Couch to 5K weeks 1-8). Reeves rule: whole body three times a week, 45-60 s rests, progress one rep at a time.
2. Engine Builder (intermediate, 5 days, gym, 8 weeks)
   Mon full-body strength (squat 5x5, bench 5x5, row 4x8), Tue conditioning (Norwegian 4x4 or a scaled Girl: Helen / Jackie / Nancy), Wed Zone 2 45 min, Thu full-body strength (deadlift 5x5, press 5x5, pull-up 4xAMRAP), Fri WOD (Cindy / Fran scaled / Fight Gone Bad) or Billat 30/30, Sat long easy session 60 min, Sun off. Froning rule: the weakest skill goes first in the week.
3. Hybrid Athlete (advanced, 6 days, gym, 12 weeks in three 4-week blocks)
   Upper / Lower strength (PHUL power days), two WOD days drawn from the benchmark list with recorded scores, two Zone 2 runs or rides, one long run building 60 to 90 min. Polarized: 80% of cardio minutes easy. Kipchoge rule: most minutes easy, 1-2 hard sessions, one long one.
4. Lean at Home (all levels, 3 strength + 2-3 cardio, no equipment to pull-up bar, rolling)
   Recommended-Routine pairs (pull-up / squat, dip / hinge, row / push-up ladders, 3x5-8, move up at 3x8) plus core triplet; bodyweight WODs (Cindy, Chelsea, Angie scaled, Murph scaled); jump-rope intervals; Couch to 5K.

## CUT programs

1. Definition 3-day (beginner, 3 days, gym or dumbbells, 8 weeks)
   Upper / Lower / Full, 3x8-12 double progression, antagonist supersets with 60 s rest, finishing with 2 high-rep pump sets. LISS 2x30 min + 8-10k steps. Deficit starts at 15%. Bumstead rule: the split that fits your week.
2. Zane Pyramid (intermediate, 4 days, gym, 9 weeks = 3 on / 1 off cycled)
   Zane's three-way split run as D1 back / biceps / forearms / abs, D2 thighs / calves / abs, D3 chest / shoulders / triceps / abs. Compounds as descending pyramids 12 / 10 / 8 / 6, isolation 12-15, 60-90 s rest, controlled negatives. Abs every session. Cardio: Tabata or Gibala 2x, LISS 1x 40 min. Zane rule: moderate load, deep negatives, never heavy for its own sake.
3. Shred Protocol (advanced, 6 days push / pull / legs, gym, 12 weeks)
   Keep a heavy top set (5-6 reps) on the first lift of every session to hold strength, then 10-14 working sets at 8-15 reps, 60 s rest, Nubret pump finisher (4 x 20 with 20-30 s rest). 2 HIIT + 2 LISS. Optional two-a-day weeks (Plitt pattern: lift morning, 30 min cardio evening). Carb cycling note (Strebl: three lower days, one higher day). MATADOR-style diet break every 4 weeks.
4. Cut at Home (all levels, 4 days, bodyweight to dumbbells, rolling)
   RR ladders at high density (90 s rest cut to 60 s), bodyweight circuits (20 min AMRAP), jump-rope Tabata, daily step target, Zone 2 walks.

## BUFFED programs

1. Golden Six (beginner, 3 days full body, gym, 8 weeks) - Arnold
   Squat 4x10, bench 3x10, chin-up 3xAMRAP, overhead press 4x10 (standing, in place of behind-the-neck for shoulder safety), barbell curl 3x10, sit-up 3-4 sets. Add 2.5 kg when all sets hit 10 (5 kg on squat). Arnold rule: earn volume on the basics; the last 3-4 reps do the work.
2. 5x5 Linear (beginner, 3 days full body, gym, 12 weeks)
   A: squat / bench / row 5x5. B: squat / press 5x5, deadlift 1x5. +2.5 kg per session (+5 kg deadlift). Three fails in a row drops 10%. Switches you to GZCLP when the drops start repeating.
3. GZCLP (beginner to intermediate, 3-4 days, gym, 12 weeks)
   T1 5x3+ > 6x2+ > 10x1+ > retest; T2 3x10 > 3x8 > 3x6; T3 3x15+ (add weight at 25). The engine runs the stages automatically from logged reps. Bjornsson rule: start blocks light, add every session, do not max often.
4. PHUL (intermediate, 4 days, gym, 10 weeks)
   Upper power / lower power (3-5 reps) and upper / lower hypertrophy (8-12). Double progression. Haney rule: 6-8 heavy reps, stop short of failure, recover.
5. 5/3/1 Boring But Big (intermediate to advanced, 4 days, gym, 3-week waves x 4)
   Training max 90%; 5s / 3s / 1s waves with the AMRAP top set, +2.5 / +5 kg per cycle, BBB 5x10 at 50-60%. Training-max test every 10 weeks, automatic reset from the estimated 1RM.
6. PPL 6-day (advanced, 6 days, gym, 8 weeks) - Coleman
   Each muscle twice a week on heavy compound basics with 1x5+ top sets, accessories 8-12, lateral raises and face pulls high rep. Yates "Blood and Guts" offered as the 4-day alternative: ramp-up sets then one all-out set per exercise, 50 minutes.
7. Buffed at Home (all levels, 3-4 days, dumbbells or bar, rolling)
   RR ladders with weighted steps (weighted pull-ups / dips, Bulgarian split squats, Nordic curls), dumbbell upper / lower, 8-12 reps double progression.
