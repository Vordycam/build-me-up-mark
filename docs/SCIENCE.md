# Evidence-based programming rules for Lean / Cut / Buffed (research notes, 2026-11-19)

## A. Resistance-training dose

Weekly sets per muscle
- Schoenfeld, Ogborn and Krieger 2017 (15 studies): hypertrophy 5.4% (<5 sets/wk), 6.6% (5-9), 9.8% (10+); graded dose-response. DOI 10.1080/02640414.2016.1210197; https://lookgreatnaked.com/blog/how-many-sets-do-you-need-to-perform-to-maximize-muscle-gains
- Renaissance Periodization landmarks (intermediates, sets/wk): maintenance about 6 when each muscle is hit 2x/wk; chest MEV 4-6 / MAV 6-16 / MRV 16-24, 2-4x/wk; quads MEV 4-6 / MAV 6-14 / MRV 14-18, 2-5x/wk; triceps MRV 18; back MEV about 10 / MAV 14-22 / MRV 25 (third-party copy). https://rpstrength.com/blogs/articles/training-volume-landmarks-muscle-growth

Rep ranges and load (NSCA Essentials table 17.7; ACSM 2009 position stand)
- Strength: 85%+ 1RM, 6 or fewer reps, 2-5 min rest. Hypertrophy: 67-85%, 6-12 reps, 1-2 min rest (longer is fine if it preserves volume). Endurance: 67% or less, 12+ reps, 30-60 s rest.
- ACSM 2009: novice/intermediate hypertrophy 70-85% 1RM, 8-12 reps, 1-3 sets; advanced 70-100%, 6-12 RM, 3-6 sets, periodized; frequency novice 2-3 d/wk full-body, intermediate 3 full-body or 4 split, advanced 4-6; tempo novice 2:4 or 1:2 (concentric:eccentric). https://www.ideafit.com/progression-models-in-resistance-training-for-healthy-adults

Proximity to failure
- Robinson 2024 meta-regression: strength similar across a wide RIR range; hypertrophy rises as sets end closer to failure. Refalo 2024 RCT: failure vs RIR gave similar quad growth in trained men. Practitioner rule: 0-3 reps in reserve on working sets, failure only on the last set of isolation work. https://biolayne.com/reps/issue-23/is-training-to-failure-overrated/

Rest
- Stronger by Science meta: no meaningful hypertrophy difference 1-2 vs 2-3 vs 3+ min; longer rest helps when it preserves volume. Barbell Medicine: 2-5 min for strength. https://www.strongerbyscience.com/rest-times-for-muscle-growth/

Frequency
- Schoenfeld 2016 (10 studies): 2x/wk per muscle beats 1x when volume is equated. https://link.springer.com/10.1007/s40279-016-0543-8

Tempo
- Schoenfeld 2015: hypertrophy similar for 0.5-8 s reps taken to failure; over 10 s is inferior.

Deloads
- Survey of 246 competitive lifters: about 6 days every 5-6 weeks; coaches usually one week, volume cut, frequency kept. https://shura.shu.ac.uk/33446/1/s40798-024-00691-y.pdf

Progression
- NSCA 2-for-2 rule: 2+ reps over target on the final set in two consecutive sessions, add 2-10%.
- Double progression: add reps until the top of the range on all sets, then +2.5 kg / 5 lb and restart at the bottom.
- Linear progression: +2.5 kg / 5 lb upper, +5 kg / 10 lb lower per session.

## B. Program templates

GZCLP (https://thefitness.wiki/routines/gzclp/)
- 4 rotating days, a rest day between. D1 squat / bench / lat pulldown; D2 OHP / deadlift / DB row; D3 bench / squat / pulldown; D4 deadlift / OHP / DB row.
- T1 5x3+ > fail > 6x2+ > fail > 10x1+ > fail > test 5RM, restart at 85%. T2 3x10 > 3x8 > 3x6 > fail > restart stage 1 at last stage-1 weight +15-20 lb. T3 3x15+; add weight when the AMRAP set hits 25.
- Increments each session: +5 lb bench / OHP, +10 lb squat / deadlift. Rest T1 3-5 min, T2 2-3, T3 1-1.5.

5/3/1 (https://thefitness.wiki/5-3-1-primer/ ; https://jimwendler.com/blogs/jimwendler-com/101077382-boring-but-big)
- Training max = 85-90% 1RM (default 90%). Week 1: 65/75/85% x 5/5/5+. Week 2: 70/80/90% x 3/3/3+. Week 3: 75/85/95% x 5/3/1+. AMRAP never to true failure.
- +5 lb press / bench, +10 lb squat / deadlift per 3-week cycle. TM test every ~10 weeks. Older every-4th-week deload (40/50/60% x5) is optional now.
- Boring But Big assistance: 5x10 at 50-60% TM.

PHUL (StrengthLog version; https://www.strengthlog.com/phul-workout-routine/)
- 4 d/wk. Upper power: bench 3-4x3-5, row 3-4x3-5, incline DB 3-4x6-10, pulldown 3-4x6-10, OHP 2-3x5-8, curl, skull-crusher. Lower power: squat 3-4x3-5, deadlift 3-4x3-5, split squat, leg curl, calf 3-4x6-10. Upper / lower hypertrophy days: 3-4x8-12. Progress +2.5 kg / 5 lb at the top of the range. Rest 2-3 min compounds, 1-2 isolation.

Push / Pull / Legs 6-day (Reddit PPL; https://liftvault.com/programs/bodybuilding/metallicadpa-ppl/)
- Pull A: deadlift 1x5+, pulldown 3x8, row 3x8, face pull 5x15, hammer curl 4x8, curl 4x8. Push A: bench 4x5, 1x5+, OHP 3x8, incline DB 3x8, pushdown 3x8, overhead extension 3x8, lateral raise 6x15. Legs: squat 2x5, 1x5+, RDL 3x8, leg press 3x8, leg curl 3x8, calf 5x8. Pull B / Push B swap row and OHP in as the main 4x5, 1x5+ lift.
- +5 lb per session on bench, OHP, row, squat; +10 lb deadlift. Accessories 8 > 12 then +5 lb. Miss reps 3 sessions > deload that lift 10%.

Upper / Lower 4-day (https://www.strengthlog.com/strengthlogs-upper-lower-program-4-days-week/)
- U1 bench 5x5, pull-up 4x10, incline DB 3x10, row 4x10. L1 squat 5x5, RDL 4x10. U2 OHP 5x5, pull-up 4x10, close-grip bench or dip 3x10, DB row 3x10. L2 deadlift 5x5, front squat 5x5. Add 2.5 kg / 5 lb once all sets hit target.

Full-body 3-day beginner (StrongLifts 5x5 / Starting Strength; https://startingstrength.com/article/programming-a-smooth-nlp)
- A: squat / bench / row 5x5. B: squat / OHP 5x5, deadlift 1x5. 3x/wk alternating. +2.5 kg (5 lb) per workout, deadlift +5 kg (10 lb). Stall > repeat; 3 consecutive fails > -10%; after 3 squat deloads switch to 3x5.

## C. Fat-loss phase (Cut and Lean)
- Rate 0.5-1% bodyweight/week, slower when leaner; about 500 kcal/day deficit; prep 2-4 months (Helms 2014). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4033492/
- Garthe 2011: 0.7%/wk kept or added lean mass vs 1.4%/wk in athletes training 4x/wk.
- Protein: 2.3-3.1 g/kg fat-free mass (Helms 2014); Refalo / Trexler / Helms 2025 meta-regression: linear dose-response up to about 3.2 g/kg bodyweight for lean, hard-dieting lifters. Fat 15-30% kcal, carbs the remainder.
- Training in a cut: keep 10+ sets/muscle/wk and keep intensity (Roth 2022); cutting volume looked worse; 12 vs 20 quad sets/wk no difference over 6 wk (Roth 2023).
- Cardio: 150-250 min/wk moderate gives modest loss; 250+ min/wk gives clinically significant loss and maintenance (ACSM 2009 Donnelly). HIIT vs moderate steady: no difference in fat mass, HIIT about 40% less time (Wewege 2017). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28401638/
- Steps: health benefit plateaus 8,000-10,000/day under 60 (Paluch 2022).
- Diet breaks: MATADOR (Byrne 2018) 2 wk at 67% maintenance alternating with 2 wk maintenance beat continuous dieting and kept about 8 kg more loss at 6 months. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5803575

## D. Cardio prescription
- Zones (% HRmax): Z1 50-60, Z2 60-70, Z3 70-80, Z4 80-90, Z5 90-100; HRmax about 220 - age. Talk test: moderate = can talk not sing; vigorous = a few words. https://health.clevelandclinic.org/exercise-heart-rate-zones-explained
- Polarized: elite endurance about 80% easy / 20% hard (Seiler 2010). Zone 2: at least 3 sessions/wk to improve (San Millan).
- Norwegian 4x4 (Helgerud 2007): 4 x 4 min at 90-95% HRmax, 3 min active recovery at 70%, 3x/wk for 8 wk; VO2max +7% vs +5.5% for 15/15.
- Tabata 1996: 7-8 x 20 s at 170% VO2max / 10 s rest, cycle, 4 d/wk + one 30-min steady day, 6 wk; VO2max and anaerobic capacity both up.
- Gibala: 4-6 x 30 s all-out / 4 min recovery, 3x/wk; practical model 10 x 60 s at 90% HRmax / 60 s easy.
- Billat 30/30: 30 s at vVO2max / 30 s at 50%, 10-20 rounds.
- Rowing: 4 x 500 m / 2 min easy rest, build reps (Concept2).
- Jump rope: 30 s on / 30 s off, then 45/15; 3-5 rounds.
- Couch to 5K: 9 weeks, 3 runs/wk (see PROTOCOLS.md).

## E. Body-composition targets and surplus
- ACE body-fat categories (men / women): essential 2-5 / 10-13; athletes 6-13 / 14-20; fitness 14-17 / 21-24; acceptable 18-24 / 25-31; obese 25+ / 32+.
- App mapping: Lean = athletes / fitness (men 10-17%, women 18-24%); Cut = athlete low end (men 6-12%, women 14-20%; stage-lean 4-6% / 10-12% is competition only); Buffed = men 12-20%, women 20-28% during a surplus (working range, not an official one).
- Surplus (Iraki 2019): +10-20% above maintenance, gain 0.25-0.5% bodyweight/wk for novice / intermediate; advanced use the low end. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6680710
- Protein for hypertrophy: breakpoint 1.62 g/kg/day (Morton 2018), practical target 1.6-2.2 g/kg.

## Flagged / not verified
- Several primary abstracts (Schoenfeld 2017, Tabata 1996, Helgerud 2007, ACSM 2009) were read through reputable summaries, not the journals.
- PHUL has no first-party source; StrengthLog's version is used.
- Zone 2 weekly-minute targets and "bulked" body-fat ranges are practitioner numbers, not primary research.
