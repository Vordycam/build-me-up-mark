## B. Workout formats

- AMRAP: as many rounds and/or reps as possible in the time; score rounds + reps. Example: Cindy.
- EMOM: a fixed set of reps starts at the top of every minute; leftover seconds are rest. Example: Chelsea.
- For time (with time cap): finish the listed work as fast as possible; at the cap the score becomes reps completed. Example: Fran, 7:00 cap.
- Rounds for time: fixed number of rounds as fast as possible. Example: Helen.
- Chipper: one long pass, all reps of one movement before the next. Example: Angie.
- Ladder: reps climb or descend each round. Examples: 21-15-9; 50-40-30-20-10.
- Tabata: 8 x (20 s work / 10 s rest) = 4 min. Example: "Tabata This!" (row, squat, pull-up, push-up, sit-up; 1:00 rest between movements; score = lowest-rep interval of each).
- Death by: escalating EMOM, 1 rep in minute 1, 2 in minute 2... until the minute's reps no longer fit. Example: death by burpees.
- Fight Gone Bad: 3 rounds of 5 one-minute stations, 1:00 rest after each round (17 min): wall-balls 20/14 lb, SDHP 75/55 lb, box jump 20 in, push press 75/55 lb, row for calories; score = total reps + calories.
Sources: https://crossfit.com/essentials/crossfit-terms-explained, https://www.crossfit.com/benchmark/tabata-this, https://www.crossfit.com/benchmark/fight-gone-bad

## C. r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine (mirror https://redditbwf.github.io/wiki/recommended_routine.html)

Schedule: 3x/week with at least one rest day between sessions.
Warm-up (5-10 min): shoulder band warm-up 5-10; squat sky reaches 5-10; wrist prep 10+; dead bugs 30 s; later arch hangs 10, support hold 30 s, an easier squat step 10, an easier hinge step 10.
Strength (40-60 min), alternate exercises within each pair, 90 s rest:
1. Pull-up progression 3x5-8 / Squat progression 3x5-8
2. Dip progression 3x5-8 / Hinge progression 3x5-8
3. Row progression 3x5-8 / Push-up progression 3x5-8
Core triplet, 60 s rest: anti-extension 3x8-12, anti-rotation 3x8-12, extension 3x8-12.
Tempo 1 s down, no pause, explode up; stop one rep short of failure.
Progression rule: hardest step you can manage for 3x5; add a rep per set each session; at 3x8 with good form move to the next step and restart at 3x5. Holds 10-30 s per set; move up when all 3 sets hit 30 s.

Ladders:
- Pull-up: scapular pulls > arch hangs > pull-up negatives > pull-ups > weighted pull-ups.
- Squat: assisted squat > squat > split squat > Bulgarian split squat > beginner shrimp > intermediate shrimp > advanced shrimp > weighted. (Pistol squat as an alternative path.)
- Dip: parallel-bar support hold > negative dips > dips > weighted dips.
- Hinge: Romanian deadlift > single-leg deadlift > banded Nordic negatives > banded Nordic curl > Nordic curl.
- Row: vertical rows > incline rows > horizontal rows > wide rows > weighted inverted rows / tuck front lever rows.
- Push-up: wall push-up > incline push-up > full push-up > diamond push-up > pseudo-planche push-up.
- Anti-extension: plank (to 3x30 s) > ab rollouts (kneeling, then standing).
- Anti-rotation: banded Pallof press (stronger band / farther away); Copenhagen plank alternate.
- Extension: reverse hyperextension; arch body or back extensions alternate.
Barbell substitution: with a barbell and rack replace the squat progression with barbell squats 3x5 and the hinge progression with Romanian deadlifts 3x8 on workouts 1 and 3 and deadlifts 3x5 on workout 2.

## E. Cardio interval sets

- Running 400 m repeats: 5 x 400 m with 90 s jog up to 10-12 x 400 m at 5K pace with 2:00 rest. https://www.active.com/running/articles/intermediate-5k-training-plan-7-weeks
- Billat 30/30: 30 s hard / 30 s easy, 12-20 reps; progress to 60/60. https://www.sportsperformancebulletin.com/training/high-intensity-training/billat-intervals-the-magic-bullet-for-your-next-pb
- Rowing: 4 x 500 m / 1:00 rest; 500 m test plan 4 x 500 m / 2:00 up to 8 x 500 m; 6 x 500 m / 1:45 each faster; pyramid 500-1000-1500-1000-500-250 m. https://www.concept2.com/training/plans/500m-erg-test
- Cycling: Norwegian 4x4 = 4 x 4 min at 90-95% HRmax / 3 min active recovery; Ronnestad 30/15 = 3 x 13 x (30 s hard / 15 s easy), 3 min between series.
- Jump rope: 30 s on / 30 s off for 3-5 min, then 10 rounds growing 30 to 60 s work; double-unders only after basics.
- Tabata: 8 x 20/10.
