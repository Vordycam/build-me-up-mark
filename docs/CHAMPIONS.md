# Champion insights - research notes (2026-11-19)

Key: [V] = confirmed on a cited page; [U] = widely repeated but not traced to a primary source. Items marked [U] get softer attribution in the app ("widely attributed") or are left out.

## Arnold Schwarzenegger - BUFFED
- Titles: Mr. Olympia 1970-75, 1980 (7x) [V]; Mr. Universe 1967-70 [U].
- Philosophy: very high volume and frequency, each body part 2-3x/week, 6 days/week, often two sessions a day; heavy basics; fixation on the last grinding reps. Beginners earn volume with a simple full-body routine first.
- Protocol: "Golden Six" full body 3x/week - squat 4x10, wide-grip bench 3x10, chin-up 3xAMRAP, behind-the-neck press 4x10, barbell curl 3x10, bent-knee sit-up 3-4 sets to failure; add sets over time. https://liftvault.com/programs/bodybuilding/arnold-schwarzenegger-workout-routine-golden-six/ Advanced: double split, 3-4 sets x 8-12, 9-12 sets per muscle per session. https://fitnessvolt.com/arnold-schwarzenegger-volume-workout/
- Quote: "The last three or four reps is what makes the muscle grow." https://fitnessvolt.com/33634/mr-olympia-champion-quotes/ [U for original interview]

## Frank Zane - CUT
- Titles: Mr. Olympia 1977, 1978, 1979 [V]; IFBB Mr. Universe 1968 [V].
- Philosophy: proportion over mass; moderate loads, strict form, descending-rep pyramids, deep controlled negatives; cut training days when overtraining showed.
- Protocol: 3-way split - Day 1 back/biceps/forearms/abs; Day 2 thighs/calves/abs; Day 3 chest/shoulders/triceps/abs; 3 on/1 off, later Day1-rest-Day2-Day3-rest. Samples: bench 12/10/8/6/4/2; squat 15/12/11/10/9/8; T-bar row 12/10/8; abs daily. https://frankzane.com/the-growth-program-then-and-now-frank-zane/
- Quote: "I never went extremely heavy in this one, but concentrated on going deep into the negative." (same URL) [V]

## Steve Reeves - LEAN (classic proportion)
- Titles: Mr. America 1947, Mr. World 1948, NABBA Mr. Universe 1950 [V]. https://stevereeves.com/bodybuilding
- Philosophy: the body as a harmonious whole; published ratios (arm 252% of wrist, chest 148% of pelvis, waist 86% of pelvis, thigh 175% of knee); whole body 3x/week, short rests, progress one rep at a time.
- Protocol: 1951 routine, full body Mon/Wed/Sat, 13 exercises, mostly 2-3 sets of 8-15, 45-60 s rests. https://gymtalk.com/?p=17485 [U exact numbers]. Ratios: https://www.ironmanmagazine.com/?p=16269
- Quote: "If a man's arms appear bigger than his head, his body is thrown out of proportion." [U]

## Serge Nubret - CUT / LEAN
- Titles: IFBB Mr. Europe 1970; NABBA Pro Mr. Universe 1976; 2nd Mr. Olympia 1975 [V].
- Philosophy: shape muscle with moderate weight, high reps and tiny rests, chasing a continuous pump rather than failure.
- Protocol: 12-20 reps, 20-30 s rest, very high set counts (16-40 sets per body part [U]). https://fitnessvolt.com/7705/serge-nubrets-workout-pump-training
- Quote: "I still don't use heavy weights - I use high reps, with very little rest between sets." (1992 interview reprint) https://ditillo2.blogspot.com/2022/10/my-first-half-century-of-training-serge.html

## Lee Haney - BUFFED
- Titles: Mr. Olympia 1984-1991 (8 straight) [V].
- Philosophy: heavy enough to grow, never so heavy that form or joints give; avoid failure; recover fully.
- Protocol: 3 on/1 off - chest+biceps; quads+hamstrings; back+shoulders; heavy sets 6-8 reps, never below 4-5. https://fitnessvolt.com/lee-haney-stimulate-dont-annihilate/
- Quote: "Exercise to stimulate, not to annihilate." https://fitnessvolt.com/33634/mr-olympia-champion-quotes/

## Dorian Yates - BUFFED
- Titles: Mr. Olympia 1992-1997 (6) [V].
- Philosophy: HIT - warm-up ramps, then one all-out working set to failure; about 50 min, 4 days/week.
- Protocol: "Blood and Guts" 4-day split - shoulders/triceps/abs; back/rear delts; chest/biceps/abs; legs. Chest: incline barbell press 12, 10, 6-8, then one all-out set of 6; machine press 10, 6-8; incline DB fly 10, 8; cable crossover 8-10. https://www.boostcamp.app/coaches/dorian-yates/dorian-yates-4-day-split [U day grouping]
- Quote: "You don't need to do more sets, more volume... get whatever your max is and do one set and you would get growth." https://fitnessvolt.com/dorian-yates-one-all-out-set-growth

## Ronnie Coleman - BUFFED
- Titles: Mr. Olympia 1998-2005 (8) [V].
- Philosophy: power-bodybuilding - heavy free-weight basics, 6 days a week, every muscle twice.
- Protocol: Mon back/biceps; Tue chest/triceps; Wed legs; Thu shoulders; Fri back/biceps; Sat chest/triceps; Sun rest. Most work 8-20 reps [U]. https://generationiron.com/ronnie-coleman-favorite-6-day-workout-split/
- Quote: "Everybody wants to be a bodybuilder but don't nobody want to lift heavy ass weights!" https://generationiron.com/10-motivational-quotes-famous-bodybuilders/

## Chris Bumstead - CUT (classic aesthetics)
- Titles: Classic Physique Olympia 2019-2024 (6 straight); retired after the Oct 2024 win [V].
- Philosophy: classic lines (small waist, V-taper, quad sweep); splits that fit life; calm through visualization, breathwork, showing up on bad days.
- Protocol: 2023 prep, 3 on/1 off - quad-dominant legs; rest; shoulders (+chest); hamstrings (+back); arms; rest. https://fitnessvolt.com/chris-bumstead-current-workout-split-2023-olympia
- Quote: "The most optimal split is going to be whatever you can line up perfectly into your routine and schedule." https://fitnessvolt.com/chris-bumstead-2024-mr-olympia-expectations-gut-health/

## Rich Froning, Mat Fraser, Tia-Clair Toomey - LEAN (engine)
- Titles: Froning 2011-14 (+6 team titles) [V]; Fraser 2016-20 (5) [V]; Toomey 2017-22, 2024, 2025 (8) [V]. https://barbend.com/news/tia-clair-toomey-orr-wins-2025-crossfit-games/
- Philosophy: build the engine with large, varied volume (Olympic lifts, gymnastics, intervals, long aerobic work); attack weaknesses first; effort is the only thing you control (Fraser's HWPO).
- Protocol (Froning): about 3 sessions/day of 1-2 h; pick the weakest skill and train it. https://coachweb.com/exercises/sport-workouts/2137/mf-interviews-crossfit-champion-rich-froning
- Quotes: Froning "The best bet is just to work on whatever you feel weakest at." Fraser "The only thing consistently within your control is your effort." https://www.boxrox.com/?p=249037

## Eddie Hall and Hafthor Bjornsson - BUFFED (strength mass)
- Titles: Hall WSM 2017; 500 kg deadlift 9 July 2016 [V]. Bjornsson WSM 2018; 501 kg deadlift 2 May 2020 [V]. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_people_who_have_broken_the_1000lb_barrier_in_the_deadlift
- Philosophy: maximal strength from the floor; start each block light and add weight weekly; eating and sleep are training.
- Protocol (Bjornsson): deadlift-centred blocks starting well below max, progressing upward; warns against going heavy too often. https://barbend.com/hafthor-bjornsson-lifting-heavy-too-often
- Quote (Bjornsson): "I like to start my programs on the lower end, and then as we progress, I'll increase the weights."

## Helmut Strebl, Greg Plitt - CUT (shredded)
- Strebl: Mr. Austria 1996; Miami Pro World Champion natural 2013, 2014 [V]; 6-day split, supersets to failure; carb cycling (3 days 150-200 g, day 4 300-400 g). https://generationiron.com/helmut-strebl-profile/
- Plitt (1977-2015): fitness model, former Army Ranger; MFT28 - 4 weeks, two sessions/day (morning mass, evening fat-burn), 11 workouts/week. https://bodybuilding.com/workout-plans/about/mft28-greg-plitts-4-week-military-fitness-trainer-by-met-rx
- Lazar Angelov: no titles, contradictory routines - leave out.

## David Goggins - LEAN (endurance mindset)
- Ex-Navy SEAL; ultramarathoner; author of Can't Hurt Me (2018).
- Philosophy: discomfort is the stimulus for the mind; the inner "governor" stops most people near 40% of capacity.
- Protocol: 4x4x48 - run or walk 4 miles every 4 hours for 48 hours. https://run247.com/race-guides/david-goggins-4x4x48-challenge-date-advice-how-to
- Quote: "Sadly, most of us give up when we've only given around 40 percent of our maximum effort." https://www.nateliason.com/notes/cant-hurt-me-david-goggins

## Eliud Kipchoge - LEAN (cardio)
- Olympic marathon gold 2016 and 2020; WR 2:01:09 Berlin 2022 (since broken) [V]; 1:59:40 Vienna 2019 (unofficial).
- Philosophy: simplicity and hard work; huge aerobic volume, most runs easy, a few hard sessions a week (polarized in practice).
- Protocol: about 200 km/week; one 30-40 km long run; 1-2 fartleks; one hard track session weekly; strength + core sessions. https://marathonhandbook.com/how-eliud-kipchoge-trains-for-a-marathon/
- Quote: "No human is limited." https://athleticsweekly.com/news/interviews/world-record-reflection-qa-with-eliud-kipchoge-1039913450/
